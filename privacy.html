Privacy Policy

Illuminated Quran Uploader · last updated 6 October 2026

The Illuminated Quran Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Illuminated Quran to publish and organise that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission, given through Google's sign-in, the tool uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • read the channel's own videos, playlists and their status;
  • set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions on the channel's own videos;
  • add the channel's videos to its playlists, and set when a video becomes public.

It does not read comments, viewers' information, or any other account's data.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and the records of each video's upload state are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Viewers

This tool collects no information about people who watch the channel. Watching the videos on YouTube is covered by YouTube's own terms and Google's privacy policy.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be withdrawn at any time, in either of two ways:

  • from the Google Account's security settings: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions; or
  • with the tool's own sign-out command, which revokes the access with Google at once and deletes the stored token.

When access is withdrawn either way, the stored token and the tool's records of the channel's videos are deleted from the owner's computer within 7 days. A request to delete stored data is honoured within 7 days. The stored video records are refreshed from YouTube at least every 30 days, or deleted.

Cookies and advertising

The tool and this website use no cookies, show no advertising and include no analytics.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: through the contact page (GitHub issues), or the channel's YouTube page, @theilluminatedquran.
