The Illuminated Quran

The Quran, surah by surah.

The Illuminated Quran is a YouTube channel of read-along recitations. Each verse appears on screen in time with the recitation, with the Arabic text, the English translation (Saheeh International) and a transliteration, and the word being recited is highlighted.

The channel is non-commercial and is not monetised.

Watch on YouTube: @theilluminatedquran

About this site

The channel's videos are published with a small private tool, the Illuminated Quran Uploader, which only its owner uses to upload and organise videos on this one channel. Its privacy policy explains what it accesses.
